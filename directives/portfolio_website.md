# Directive: Portfolio Website Implementation (Sasmitha Nimesh - ANN & Math Theme)

## 1. Goal & Overview
Build an editorial, high-aesthetic AI Engineer & Full-Stack Developer portfolio website for **Sasmitha Nimesh**, inspired by the [Canada Webflow Template](https://az-canada.webflow.io/).

The visual aesthetic incorporates an interactive **Artificial Neural Network (ANN) and Complex Mathematical Theories** dynamic canvas background in an electric **Black & Blue** palette. Synaptic nodes, forward/backprop electrical pulses, mathematical coordinate lattices, and floating theoretical formulas (gradient vectors, activation functions, loss gradients, loss surfaces) drift dynamically in response to mouse movement at 60fps.

The site is a pure engineering showcase featuring real projects (Student Performance Analysis in R, Smart Shopping Buddy IoT cart, AgroVision Smart Farming, Connect Lanka Tourism), technical arsenal, downloadable resume, direct GitHub/LinkedIn case study links, and custom magnetic cursor.

## 3. Design Tokens & Visual Hierarchy
- **Background**: `#03060d` (deep cyber obsidian) & `#08142f` (midnight blue radial gradient)
- **Synaptic Accent**: `#38bdf8` (Electric Sky Blue)
- **Pulse Accent**: `#64ffda` (Synaptic Action Potential Glow)
- **Deep Synapse**: `#2563eb` (Royal Cobalt Blue)
- **Text**: `#f0f5ff` (Primary) & `#8ea3cc` (Muted)
- **Typography**: `Syne`, `Space Grotesk`, and `Manrope`
- **Dynamic Background**:
  - Full-screen `<canvas id="ann-math-canvas">` with dynamic device-pixel-ratio scaling

  - Drifting mathematical equations: $\nabla L = \partial L / \partial W$, $\sigma(z)$, $y = f(W \cdot x + b)$, $W := W - \eta \nabla L$, etc.
  - Interactive mouse excitation and repulsion physics
